## Accounting

Report approached, consented, started, completed, withdrew, stopped, and technical-failure counts by
cohort. Explain every exclusion and link it to the session log.

## Task results

For each cohort and task, report unassisted/assisted/failed counts, median time, median ease rating,
and observed barriers. Small formative samples must not be presented as population percentages
without counts.

## Safety comprehension

Report every critical misunderstanding, the exact de-identified behavior or quote, root-cause
hypothesis, design response, and retest result. Passing requires zero unresolved critical
misunderstandings in the final formative round.

## Accessibility and qualitative findings

Group findings by keyboard, screen reader, vision/text size, cognition/health literacy, motor use,
and language. Separate observed behavior from participant opinion and researcher inference.

## Deviations and failures

List every protocol deviation, outage, partial session, missing field, and analysis change.

## Conclusion boundary

State what the study supports about usability and comprehension. Explicitly state that it does not
establish clinical accuracy, safety, effectiveness, regulatory status, or real-world outcomes.
