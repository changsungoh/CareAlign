# Consent script and moderator guide

**Protocol:** CA-UX-001 v0.1-draft. Adapt only after ethics/privacy review.

## Pre-session consent script

“CareAlign is an unvalidated research prototype. Today you will use fictional medication records;
please do not share your own health information. The tool does not give medical advice or decide
which instruction is correct. We are testing the interface, not you. Participation is voluntary,
you may skip any question or stop at any time, and doing so has no penalty. [State approved data,
recording, retention, compensation, contacts, and complaints language here.] Do you understand the
study and agree to participate?”

Document affirmative consent using the institution-approved mechanism before starting. This draft
is not a substitute for an approved participant information sheet or consent form.

## Moderator rules

- Confirm the participant sees the synthetic-data notice.
- Ask them to think aloud without revealing the desired answer.
- Use neutral prompts: “What do you think this means?” and “What would you do next?”
- Do not say “correct,” “safe,” or “resolved” during task observation.
- Do not collect personal medication experiences. Redirect: “Please keep this about the fictional
  case.”
- Record assistance, technical faults, unexpected behavior, and exact critical statements.
- Apply stop rules immediately and record the event without identifiable health details.

## Debrief

Repeat that all records were fictional, CareAlign is not medical advice, and the session does not
show the tool is clinically safe or effective. Provide the approved study contact and withdrawal
process. If the participant raises a real medication concern, instruct them to contact a qualified
healthcare professional or appropriate urgent service; do not interpret their medication record.
