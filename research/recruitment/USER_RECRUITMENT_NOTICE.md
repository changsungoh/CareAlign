## Short notice

### Help evaluate a medication-instruction comparison prototype

CareAlign is seeking adults for a 30–45 minute formative usability session using **fictional,
synthetic medication instructions only**. We are studying whether the interface is understandable,
not evaluating your health knowledge or providing medical advice.

We are seeking:

- adults who can give informed consent and use the study language; and
- either people willing to provide a patient/caregiver perspective or healthcare professionals
  familiar with medication reconciliation.

You will complete fixed tasks and comment on the interface. **Do not send or bring medical records,
medication lists, diagnoses, screenshots, or any other patient information.** There is no clinical
assessment or treatment recommendation.
