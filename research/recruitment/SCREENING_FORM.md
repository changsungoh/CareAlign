# Eligibility screening form draft

**Study ID:** CA-UX-001  
**Do not deploy before ethics/privacy determination.**

Collect the minimum information needed for eligibility and sampling. Do not ask for health history,
diagnoses, medication use, patient records, national identifiers, date of birth, or employer name.

## Screening questions

1. Are you 18 years of age or older? **Yes / No**
2. Are you able to understand the participant information and provide consent in the study
   language? **Yes / No**
3. Which study perspective best describes you?
   - Adult member of the public / patient-caregiver perspective
   - Healthcare professional involved in medication reconciliation
   - Neither / prefer not to say
4. If healthcare professional, select the broad role category:
   - Pharmacist
   - Prescribing clinician
   - Nurse or care-transition professional
   - Other relevant healthcare role
   - Prefer not to say
5. Are you willing to use only fictional study scenarios and avoid entering any real patient or
   personal health information? **Yes / No**
6. Do you need a reasonable accessibility accommodation to take part? **Yes / No / Prefer not to
   say**
7. If yes, describe only the accommodation needed; do not provide a diagnosis. **[Optional text]**
8. Scheduling contact email: **[Collect in the approved system and separate from study data]**

## Eligibility logic

- Include only adults answering yes to questions 1, 2, and 5.
- Assign the cohort using question 3; do not infer it from health information.
- Use question 4 only to monitor the predeclared professional sample.
- An accommodation request does not make a person ineligible; plan access before scheduling.
- Do not retain ineligible responses longer than the approved recruitment period.

No automatic rejection message should reveal sensitive inference. Use a neutral message: “Thank
you for your interest. Based on the study's current eligibility criteria, we cannot schedule you for
this round.”
