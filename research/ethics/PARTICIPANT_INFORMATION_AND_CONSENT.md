# Participant information and consent draft

**Study:** Understanding a synthetic medication-instruction comparison prototype
**Study ID:** CA-UX-001
**Version:** 0.1-draft
**Status:** Do not distribute until approved or formally determined

## Invitation

You are invited to take part in a research study about how people understand and use CareAlign, a
research prototype. Please read this information before deciding. Ask any questions you have.
Taking part is voluntary.

## Why is the study being done?

CareAlign compares fictional care instructions and highlights wording that may need confirmation.
It does not decide which instruction is correct and does not provide medical advice. We want to
find confusing wording, unsafe misunderstandings, and accessibility problems in the interface.

## What will happen?

If you agree, you will join one session lasting about 30–45 minutes. You will use fixed fictional
scenarios, explain what you think the interface means, complete several tasks, and rate how easy
they were. We may ask follow-up questions about the interface. We will not ask you to disclose your
diagnoses, medicines, treatment decisions, or patient records.

Do not enter real patient or personal health information. If that happens, the moderator will stop
the task and follow the approved deletion/incident procedure.

Audio, video, or screen recording: **No recording is planned.** If recording is later approved, you
will receive a revised information sheet and a separate choice. Declining recording will not affect
your ability to participate unless the approved protocol explicitly states otherwise.

## Are there risks or benefits?

Possible risks include mild frustration, discomfort discussing medication-error scenarios,
accidental disclosure of personal information, or misunderstanding the prototype as advice. You
may skip a question or stop. The moderator cannot answer personal treatment questions and will
direct urgent concerns to appropriate local clinical or emergency services.

You may not benefit directly. Your feedback may help improve the safety and clarity of the
prototype. Participation does not provide medical review or clinical care.

consent details are kept separately from observations. Reports may include aggregate results and
short de-identified quotations. No raw participant data, signature, personal email, or identifiable
